### Unveiling Structural Linear Dependencies Of Handwritten Digits

This is my project regarding the research project of mine that demonstrates the linear dependencies between different fragemnts of geometric structures of handwritten digits from MNIST dataset, aiming to unveil the reproducabiliity of digits in terms of different parts of their structure. You can view the essay here on my [personal website](https://blog-kircertas-projects.vercel.app/posts/academica/essays/unveiling_structural_linear_dependencies_essay_01/).
